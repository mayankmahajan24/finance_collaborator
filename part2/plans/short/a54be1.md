# a54be1

**Hypothesis.** Primary claim, gated first: for seat-based SaaS, YoY panel-MAU growth for the client-facing app is a contemporaneous, unbiased-up-to-scale proxy for YoY billable-seat growth. Secondary (conditional): ARR ≡ seats × revenue-per-seat, so the nowcast beats naive persistence proportionally to the seat share of ARR-growth variance. Kill criterion: gate failure yields a negative result plus a taxonomy of why, not a MAU-versus-revenue fallback.

**Data.** US/Israel-listed SaaS with per-seat list pricing and a distinguishable app host; exclude consumption-metered, transacted, end-customer portals. Gate sample = seat-like disclosers: ADP pays-per-control; Paycom/Paylocity clients × employees per client; Dayforce live customers and employees on platform; Dropbox paying users; Atlassian customers and seat-tier mix; Asana/monday.com customers above spend thresholds; Smartsheet ACV per domain customer; former S-1 disclosers; reseller price lists, Microsoft/Google marketplace tiers, G2/Gartner deployment fields (sanity checks only). Primary plus replication panel vendor (Similarweb, Comscore, M Science, Yipit-style) plus mobile (Sensor Tower, data.ai): monthly uniques, devices, sessions on authenticated post-login paths, stored as point-in-time vintages. As-reported fundamentals with dates: ARR or analogue (cRPO, subscription revenue, billings), seat/user/employee/customer counts, NRR, pricing changes, fiscal calendar, FX. Pre-print consensus snapshots. Scraped quarterly tier prices (Wayback).

**Methodology.** Calibrate: devices→users via vendor device graph or per-company deflator; winsorize MoM changes at 1/99 within company; inspect >25% breaks; coverage ratio (panel MAU/disclosed seats) trended per company: >~10%/yr drift kills level work, residual SD >~0.15 drops the company; fiscal quarter-average primary (pre-committed), final-month robustness. Gate, in YoY log differences, pooled with company fixed effects: g^seat=α_i+β·g^mau+γ_t+ε (γ_t common time effect, else a macro control: YoY US software/tech employment). OLS, Driscoll–Kraay SEs at Newey–West lag 4, 2,000-draw company-block bootstrap. Diagnostics: leads/lags one–two quarters (a lead becomes the nowcast core); negative-growth interaction; paid-surface split (admin console, SSO hosts), pipeline-wide if β and correlation rise. On a pass: Δ₄ln(ARR)=Δ₄ln(Seats)+Δ₄ln(ARR per seat), residual where disclosed, modeled where not; publish Var(g^ARR)=Var(g^seat)+Var(g^price)+2Cov per company and pooled, before model results; scope to seats where price/mix dominates; flag negative Cov. Nowcast Δ₄ln(ARR)_{i,t} five business days after quarter end, plus t+1: α_i+φ·Δ₄ln(ARR)_{t−1}+β₀·g^mau_t+β₁·g^mau_{t−1}+δ·Δ₄ln(price index)_t. Pooled fixed-effects ridge (penalty by nested CV on training folds only), then empirical-Bayes shrinkage toward pooled, weighted by within- over between-company variance. Keep the lagged dependent variable; add asymmetry where diagnosed; nothing else.

**Validation.** Gate needs all four: β̂ positive, nonzero at 5% under the block bootstrap, 90% interval excluding zero; β̂ in [0.5,1.5], β=1 tested separately; within-company partial correlation (time effects out) ≥0.45 pooled, positive for two-thirds of firms; halves within ~one bootstrap SE, sign holding; leave-one-out moving β̂ <30% and not flipping significance. Baselines: growth persistence; damped AR(1), pooled decay (the real baseline); pre-print consensus. Expanding walk-forward; no fold using vintages, restatements or hyperparameters postdating the forecast date; four-quarter tuning embargo. Report RMSE (growth points), MAE, RMSE ratios; Diebold–Mariano with Harvey–Leybourne–Newbold correction at NW lag 4 and Clark–West, block-bootstrapped by company. Success: ≥15% RMSE reduction versus damped persistence, Clark–West p<0.05, directional accuracy on growth-rate change >60%; else not demonstrated. Plus leave-one-company-out prediction; surprise-space regression of consensus surprise on model-minus-consensus (positive significant slope, >55% sign hits).

**Pitfalls.** Contracted seats versus active users (seats fall only at renewal; report downside β). Free tiers (paid-surface proxy, else exclude). Deskless and dormant seats. Enterprise blindness — managed devices, VPNs, SSO, desktop clients (coverage-trend warning, second vendor). Panel restatements and tagging breaks (vintages day one). Domain and product changes (event log, break tests). Disclosure endogeneity (keep every past discloser's full window; test β in a regime's last four quarters). Small N and specification search (pre-register thresholds, model, baseline; report specification count). ARR not standardized (redefinitions are breaks). FX and errors correlated through common tech headcount (constant currency or FX term).
