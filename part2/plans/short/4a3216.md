# 4a3216

**Hypothesis.** Opaque institution IDs in a European retail panel are identifiable: the institution→fingerprint map is injective for much of the market, more in concentrated (NL, SE) than fragmented markets (DE, AT, IT). Null: fingerprints overlap (350 Sparkassen, Spanish ex-cajas), identification being threshold-fitted noise. Criterion fixed in advance: share of panel deposit volume assigned at ≥95% precision on held-out institutions; below 40% the country is not identifiable.

**Registers.** `registers.yaml` splits variables into A (panel inputs) and B (anchors — scoring only, never feature building, weighting, calibration or selection); CI fails if B reaches features.

**Data.** Panel, ≥18 months per country: customer ID, provider and counterparty institution IDs, daily timestamp, signed amount, MCC, merchant name, transaction type; age, sex, income band, NUTS-3 postcode. ≥30,000 customers per country, 100,000+ Germany. Anchors: ECB RIAD/MFI register (household-deposit holders, LEI-joined, quarter-versioned); deposit share — ECB CBD2, Bundesbank, DNB, Banco de España, Banca d'Italia, ACPR/Banque de France, KNF, EBA Transparency Exercise; footprints — ECB Payments Statistics, OSM `amenity=bank`/`atm` operator/brand tags via Overpass at NUTS-3 (coverage as known missingness), branch directories; tariffs — *Preis- und Leistungsverzeichnis*, tarievenoverzicht, *folleto de tarifas*; benefit calendars — Kindergeld by Kindergeldnummer digit, AOW/toeslagen, CAF, Seguridad Social, INPS; growth disclosures — N26, Revolut, bunq, Nickel, ING, incumbents.

**Method.** Fingerprints per country/ID/quarter: `n_share` (raked to Eurostat NUTS-2 × age × sex × income-decile margins), `geo` (Laplace-smoothed NUTS-3 shares), `age_dist`/`inc_dist`, `payday` (plus salary-only: recurring, same counterparty, ≥60% of inflow), `benefit_mix`, `fee_signature` (modal recurring debit, cents), `scheme_mix`, `growth`, `atm_geo`. Cluster IDs sharing customers, fee signature, geography; roster group → legal entity → brand. `S[i,r]` sums log-likelihood ratios over size (`log10(n_share/deposit_share)`, Gaussian, μ_c absorbing panel skew, σ_c trained), geography (Jensen–Shannon divergence vs branch footprint, logistic-mapped), fee (exact-match bonus, heavy contradiction penalty), demographics (Wasserstein vs published profiles or type prior), growth correlation, timing. Assign jointly: min-cost flow, per-ID abstain sink at τ_c, capacities >1 for multi-ID institutions, not-in-roster sink; point estimate by Jonker–Volgenant (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`, augmented matrix); marginals by Sinkhorn (ε matched to a Gibbs sampler's entropy on a small country) or Metropolis. Accept only if `max p ≥ 0.80`, margin ≥ 0.25, ≥3 comparators favouring, ≥300 customers that quarter; else `UNKNOWN` (`insufficient_evidence`, `degenerate_class`, `no_roster_candidate`, `anchor_conflict`). Thresholds recalibrated by Venn–Abers or split-conformal at 95% precision.

**Validation.** Labels: consented survey (500 respondents/country), provider-revealed seeds, unambiguous public cases; 25–60 per country, below 15 unvalidatable. Holdout unit is the institution: leave-one-institution-out, refitting weights, μ_c, σ_c, geography logistic and calibration in-fold, roster row still present (nested CV). Then leave-one-country-out: fit four, apply to the fifth on its own anchors; report both and the gap. Metrics: precision, recall, coverage unweighted and deposit-volume-weighted, risk–coverage curve, top-3 accuracy, type-level confusion. Pilots: Netherlands (if it fails there, stop), Germany, Spain, France, Poland. Controls first: permuted fingerprints must score at chance; three phantom IDs must abstain; dropping the largest comparator must degrade gracefully.

**Pitfalls.** Vendor shares themselves panel-derived — regulator/institution primaries, documented provenance, no undisclosed methodologies. Panel skew read as noise — per-type selection term, post-stratification, drop size comparator if residual skew unstable. Anchor vintage amid consolidation (CaixaBank/Bankia, Unicaja/Liberbank, mutual mergers) — quarterly versioning, temporal joins, mergers as roster transitions. Degeneracy — abstain with a candidate set from near-tie Sinkhorn marginals; class size reported. Truth outside the roster (BaaS sponsors, passported entities) — keep the sink, priored from e-money share. Tourism/commuting distortion (Balearics, Côte d'Azur, Tyrol, borders) — downweight those NUTS-3 regions. Correlated timing features — comparator covariance, multivariate scoring. Threshold overfitting — nested CV, bootstrap over institutions not transactions. Governance is gate zero: licence and GDPR clearance, no individual-level output, minimum cells (300 customers per institution, 50 per geography cell), agreed crosswalk access; if forbidden, stop.
