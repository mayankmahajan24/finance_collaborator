# 4215a5

**Hypothesis.** H1: within industry-and-size peer groups, tone above peers' recent calls — net of earnings surprise, announcement return, own baseline — predicts positive risk-adjusted returns over 1–3 months versus peers. H2: content is in unscripted Q&A and habitual-tone deviation, not prepared remarks. H3: negative deviations predict more strongly, more persistently. Null: peer-relative tone adds nothing beyond SUE, 3-day announcement return, momentum, style factors.

**Data.** S&P Global Market Intelligence transcripts (Xpressfeed/Snowflake), quarterly calls 2015–2024, historical Russell 3000; speaker-blocks/roles/presentation-Q&A markers; StreetEvents subsample cross-check. Minute-level call start, pre-open/intraday/post-close flag, else 8-K press-release timestamp flagged imputed. CRSP daily/monthly, codes 10/11, NYSE/AMEX/Nasdaq, explicit dlret. Compustat quarterly. I/B/E/S Detail History: SUE off median estimate 90 days pre-call, unadjusted per-share, matched split basis; actual EPS also I/B/E/S. French factors (Mkt-RF/SMB/HML/RMW/CMA/MOM), q5/Stambaugh-Yuan/Pastor-Stambaugh. Peers: GICS 8-digit primary, TNIC and FF48 throughout, size terciles at prior-June NYSE breakpoints. CRSP volume/price; Markit borrow, else exclude bottom-decile/sub-$5 names from the short leg (upper bound). Point-in-time merges (RDQ, historical membership/GICS); untimestampable variables dropped.

**Methodology.** Parse structured transcripts keeping speaker-ID/role/title/employer/section/turn-index/text; rebuild question-answer pairs; drop operator boilerplate, regex-detected safe-harbor block, sub-five-token turns; normalize titles to CEO/CFO/other-executive/IR/analyst/operator, CEO and CFO modeled separately. Base deberta-v3-large; FinBERT baseline to beat; domain-adaptive MLM, ~1.5–2B transcript tokens, 2–3 epochs, 15% whole-word masking, lr 1e-5 with warmup, 2023–2024 excluded. Shared encoder, two heads: 3-way tone (Financial PhraseBank 100%/75% subsets, SemEval-2017 Task 5) and binary hedging. 6,000 stratified sentences, two annotators plus adjudicator, codebook, Krippendorff alpha >0.7; ~150k LLM silver labels from that codebook, kept above 0.75 macro-F1; train silver, final pass gold. Encoder never fine-tuned on returns. Per call, by prepared/Q&A and CEO/CFO/all-management: tone=(Σp_pos−Σp_neg)/n_sentences from probabilities; uncertainty=hedged share; answer_evasion=question-answer embedding cosine + non-answer rate (<25 words or deferral phrase); numeric_density=numerals per hundred words; Loughran-McDonald tone/uncertainty as benchmarks. Δtone versus prior four calls (per speaker; reset and flag on CFO change); demean by equal-weighted peer Δtone over 45 trailing days, divide by peer cross-sectional SD, five-peer minimum else escalate to broader industry with flag; regress monthly cross-sections on SUE/(−1,+1)-return/guidance-change-indicators/log-size/book-to-market/12-1-momentum/idio-vol. Residual = signal.

**Portfolio.** Enter at t+1 close; t+2 conservative, same-day close aggressive bound. Overlapping Jegadeesh-Titman cohorts, K∈{1,2,3}, primary horizon 3 months, decay at 1/2/6/12. Quintiles on residual within peer group then pooled, EW and VW (VW headline); call-level Fama-MacBeth, Newey-West 3 lags. Long-short against CAPM/FF3/Carhart-4/FF5/FF5+MOM (headline)/q5/short-term-reversal; alpha/t-stat/loadings/IR/max-drawdown/%-positive-months. Costs: $0.0015/share, half TAQ time-weighted spread (or Corwin-Schultz), 10×σ_daily×sqrt(size/ADV) bp impact, 5%-of-20-day-median-ADV cap, Markit borrow or 40bp above size median, rest excluded. Gross and net alpha, break-even cost (<30bp = paper result), capacity where net alpha halves.

**Validation.** Split fixed day one: develop 2015–2020, select 2021–2022, touch 2023–2024 once; evaluation script pre-written; primary specification (one signal, horizon, weighting) pre-registered in a dated commit. Expanding-window walk-forward: fit through Y, trade Y+1. Model quality separately: macro/per-class F1 on gold, confusion matrix, reliability diagram, ECE, sector/year stability. Ablation: LM/FinBERT/domain-adapted-DeBERTa/+Q&A-separation/+speaker-tags/+peer-relativization/+orthogonalization. Robustness: three peer definitions; pre-COVID/2020/post-2022; size buckets; March-2020 drop; sector-/exchange-neutral variants; 1,000-draw within-date shuffle; next-call-tone-on-prior-returns placebo. Harvey-Liu-Zhu t>3.0, deflated Sharpe, count of specifications tried.

**Pitfalls.** Post-earnings drift in disguise: decile-dummy, not linear, announcement-return controls. Timestamps recording publication not call time; audit 200 calls against EDGAR 8-Ks. Survivorship in transcript coverage; annual CRSP reconciliation, coverage by size-decile/year. Pretraining overlapping the test window; rerun with earlier-cutoff base, holdout transcripts out of MLM. Recycled prepared remarks; track quarter-over-quarter cosine, control for it, sudden drops as candidate signal. Language drift; walk-forward retraining, per-year standardization, anchor-set diagnostic. Managers writing for machines (Cao, Jiang, Wang, Yang); test 2023–2024 alpha against 2015–2018. Small peer groups; fallback above 20% makes TNIC primary. Crowding and costs make break-even a primary result. Possible zero: a clean null is a real output; failure mode is week-eight t-stat hunting.
