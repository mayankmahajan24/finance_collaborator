**Hypothesis.** Tone in management's Q&A answers predicts next-quarter peer-relative return incrementally to the earnings surprise, announcement reaction, and guidance. Expected null: real in 2004-2010, since arbitraged to an incremental t under 2.

**Days 1-3, no data or code.** Four-page memo plus frozen pre-registration. Anchor: Price-Doran-Peterson-Bliss (2012). Also Loughran-McDonald (2011) word lists, Mayew-Venkatachalam vocal affect, Larcker-Zakolyukina, Bochkay-Hales-Sengupta, Hassan et al., Cohen-Malloy-Nguyen, Garcia-Hu-Rohrer, Frankel-Jennings-Lee, Ke-Kelly-Xiu, and Lopez-Lira-Tang (2023) plus Kim-Muhn-Nikolaev (2024) on look-ahead contamination. A full day on decay: McLean-Pontiff (2016), 26% out-of-sample, 32% post-publication; prior of one-third the published coefficient, then estimate the decay curve directly. Half a day of vendor calls — S&P Global Textual Data Analytics, LSEG MarketPsych, RavenPack/Bigdata, Bloomberg, AlphaSense, Aiera, Alexandria, Brain, SESAMm, absorbed Prattle/Amenity — asked seat cost for point-in-time history to 2010 and client count; availability implies crowding. Day 3: git-commit the pre-registration (regression, tone measure, universe, sample, peers, controls, standard errors, kill thresholds, robustness list) before touching returns; later ideas are exploratory, outside the gate.

**Data.** Russell 3000 point-in-time, calls January 2015 to the latest complete quarter (~90,000 firm-quarters), ≥5 peers, price ≥$5. Capital IQ transcripts via WRDS with publication timestamps, not call dates. I/B/E/S SUE (actual EPS minus 90-day median forecast, scaled by dispersion; price-scaled variant) and I/B/E/S Guidance (change in full-year EPS midpoint versus prior issuance, price-scaled, initiation/withdrawal indicators). CRSP returns and delisting returns, Compustat with reporting lag, point-in-time GICS sub-industry, Hoberg-Phillips TNIC-3 as alternative peers.

**Days 4-10, one specification.** Tone = LM positive minus negative over total words, management Q&A only, speaker tags parsed, analyst turns dropped, LM negation handling; signal is the within-firm change versus prior four calls; winsorise 1/99, decile within quarter. Primary: pooled panel of peer-relative return over [+2,+63] from the transcript timestamp on tone decile, controlling for SUE decile, CAR[-1,+1], scaled guidance revision and indicators, log size, book-to-market, 12-1 momentum, Amihud illiquidity, analyst dispersion, turnover, prior-quarter tone level; peers equal-weighted GICS sub-industry, same window; industry-by-quarter fixed effects; errors two-way clustered by firm and quarter (Petersen 2009), plus Fama-MacBeth with Newey-West lags equal to the horizon. Gate: t on tone in the full specification; an effect only in [0,+1] fails. Days 6-7 replicate 2004-2010, requiring a t near 3. Day 10: monthly-rebalanced long-short, top-minus-bottom decile within sub-industry, equal- and value-weighted, 60-day holds, overlapping tranches, gross and net of 20bp round-trip, FF5, momentum, short-term reversal exposures.

**Kill criterion, all four.** (1) Tone t ≥ 2.5 clustered, rising to 2.9 by Bonferroni if three tone variants are reported (Harvey-Liu-Zhu 2016). (2) Net spread ≥3% annualised, IR ≥0.4. (3) Correct sign in ≥7 of 10 years, t above 2.0 excluding March-December 2020. (4) Bottom size quintile <40% of spread, sign in ≥3 of 4 size quartiles. Pass: days 11-14 build scope — infrastructure, latency, capacity, versus licensing the vendor score. Fail: two-page negative memo naming residual hypotheses, likely the audio channel.

**Validation.** Placebo over [-63,-3] must show no incremental coefficient; shuffled speaker labels must kill it; on a one-month S&P or MarketPsych trial, rank correlation above 0.7 means commodity duplication and the vendor score becomes the control.

**Pitfalls.** Surprise contamination (omit SUE/CAR, get PEAD); timestamp leakage, worst in small illiquid names; sector loading, checked in extreme-decile composition; LM dictionary drift, differenced out by the within-firm change; boilerplate safe-harbour language; coverage bias, since transcript availability tracks size and analyst following; LLM look-ahead (models trained through 2024 have read the outcomes), so any LLM variant is exploratory, stratified 20% subsample, scrambled-date control, outside the gate; overlapping windows inflating t-statistics, handled by clustering and Newey-West. After day three no new specification becomes primary. A t of 2.1 is a kill.
