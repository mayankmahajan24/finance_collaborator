# 61aec8

Hypothesis: management sounding evasive about the vendor licence renegotiation splits into two testable claims. H1 (measurement): on vendor/procurement topics, the current call's answers are elevated on an evasiveness score relative to (a) the same speakers' answers on other topics in the same call, and (b) the same speakers' vendor-topic answers in the trailing eight calls, at ≥1.5 SD above the firm's own trailing baseline (SD from that firm's call-to-call variation, not cross-sectional). H2 (predictive content): across a broad panel, that elevation predicts negative gross-margin surprises one to four quarters out, conditional on public information — a 1-SD increase in within-firm ΔScore predicts a GM surprise ≥15bp more negative with |t|≥2.5 out-of-sample, plus ≥5pp improvement over base rate in calling the surprise's sign. The publishable null is H1 true, H2 false — caginess reflecting NDA/legal constraints with no informational content; this is the modal, not a failure, outcome.

Data: transcripts from S&P Capital IQ/Refinitiv StreetEvents (2005–present, US issuers >$300m cap, with speaker role and stable person ID for per-executive baselines; drop translated/truncated calls); a 200-call overlap sample from a second vendor for a source-robustness check. Outcomes from Visible Alpha (GM/COGS consensus; IBES/FactSet EPS is not a substitute), Compustat quarterly (realised financials, purchase-obligations tables from 10-Ks), IBES (60-day post-call GM revisions), CRSP (CAR around next print, drift). Case-specific: purchase-obligations tables, Item 1A supplier language redlines, vendor filings/pricing, >10% customer disclosure, job postings/capitalised software. Tools: Loughran–McDonald, Brysbaert concreteness norms, spaCy, one pinned embedding model, one pinned LLM at temp 0.

Methodology: segment Q&A into exchanges (question + management turns until next analyst); tag vendor exchanges via keyword pass plus embedding similarity to seed sentences, threshold calibrated on 500 hand-labels for precision ≥0.85/recall ≥0.75. Measure five families per management turn: hedging (modals, approximators, evidentials, parse-filtered), deflection (LLM three-way label anchored to 1,000 hand-labels, κ≥0.70, classifier agreement ≥0.80, majority of 3 runs; features include Jaccard overlap, redirect markers, number-dodge), specificity (numeral/entity density, time-anchored commitments, concreteness), normalised length (answer/question word ratio), interaction (follow-ups, speaker handoffs). Audio prosody as optional extension only. Baseline: within-call cross-topic difference (de-styling), then within-firm change vs trailing four quarters scaled by firm SD, then cross-sectional standardisation within quarter/sector. Require ≥4 prior calls per speaker; fall back to firm-level baseline on turnover, flagged low-confidence. Combine via equal-weighted mean (pre-registered primary spec); separately fit elastic-net logistic weights and require sign agreement and rank-correlation >0.7. Block-bootstrap (2,000 draws) per call; require ≥3 vendor exchanges and ≥150 words or report "insufficient evidence."

Historical test: ~40,000 firm-quarter panel. Regress GMsurprise(t+1) on ΔScore with firm and quarter FE, controls (market cap, GM level/trend, sector PPI beta, inventory change, revenue surprise, whole-call negative-word share, question/exchange counts, analyst FE), two-way clustered SEs plus Driscoll–Kraay. Secondary outcomes: cumulative GM change t+1–t+4, downgrade probability, IBES revisions, CAR. Decile-sort portfolio, sector/size-neutral, report monotonicity.

Validation: blind human rating (200 exchanges, ρ≥0.6); placebo topics (FX, headcount, buybacks) and placebo outcome (revenue surprise); strict out-of-sample split (fit 2005–2016, test 2017–present, no refitting, no look-ahead fields); reverse-causality control (condition on GM surprise/stock reaction at t); known-outcome falsification (~40 bad/~40 benign renegotiation episodes, pre-resolution calls only); transcript-source correlation must exceed 0.9 or drop affected components.

Pitfalls: question-complexity confound; contract topics are inherently conditional, hence within-topic baseline as primary; legal-constraint mimics guilt (test via post-conclusion calls); signal decay post-2018 as IR adapts; multiple-comparisons control via pre-registration and Romano–Wolf; effective N smaller than headline N — compute power beforehand; report base rates alongside lift; report margin-prediction and return-prediction outcomes side by side, since a signal with no return predictability is correct but worthless.
