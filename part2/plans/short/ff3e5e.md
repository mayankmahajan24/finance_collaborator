# ff3e5e

**Hypothesis.** H1: conditional on total speculative wallet, a within-user rise in Kalshi share `K/(K+DK+FD)` cuts DK deposits that month and the next by more than the mechanical amount. H2: category substitution drops FD too; DK down, FD up is brand switching. H3: flat total wallet with rotating composition = cannibalization, rising total = expansion. H4: displacement concentrated in long-tenured high-spend users, not new low-spend ones.

**Data.** User-month card-and-bank panel, Jan 2023–latest complete month; full outflow ledger with consumption. Hand-audited descriptor dictionary: Kalshi variants; DK sportsbook/DFS/casino separate columns summed for headline; likewise FD; 500-transaction drift audit. Two attribution gaps quantified: broker-distributed Kalshi (Robinhood, Webull) has no descriptor: test adopter concentration among non-brokerage users, report the undercount; sportsbooks' own late-2025 prediction markets (DK/Railbird, FD/CME, dates verified in filings): window ends the month before DK's launch, later months a separate flagged regime. Fields: gross deposits, payouts, net funding (preferred), deposit count, active days, state, DK tenure, pre-period wallet, completeness flags, payroll proxy. Filters: payroll/recurring-bill anchor ≥10 of 12 months; ≥6 pre- and ≥6 post-adoption months; ≥12 for never-adopters; DK-legal states only, rest placebo. Pre/post sports-contract (2025) regimes estimated separately.

**Methodology.** `ΔDK = β·Δshare + α_u + δ_t + Γ'X` with state × month controls; user-clustered SEs, Driscoll–Kraay (4 lags), wild bootstrap (999) over months. Three regressors: raw share (upper bound only); primary `K_t/W̄_u`, W̄ = fixed mean wallet six months pre-first-Kalshi; leave-out share on trailing three-month sportsbook averages. Mechanical null set analytically and by permutation (1,000 within-user DK reshuffles); pass only if β < permuted 5th percentile. `ppmlhdfe` (user, month FE) leads, then by-month 99th-percentile-winsorized OLS, then asinh; never log(x+1). Donut: drop the adoption month, compare t+1..t+6 with t−6..t−1; two DK lags; Arellano–Bond robustness. IV `Z = affinity_u × V_t`, V = national non-sports Kalshi volume, affinity fit on a 50% holdout from pre-period covariates; F > 20, Kleibergen–Paap. Cross-check: Callaway–Sant'Anna event study, first Kalshi use vs not-yet-adopters, matched on pre-period DK decile, tenure, state, pre-trend slope; not TWFE. Credible only if β × mean Δshare matches the ATT within ~2×, else the event study wins. Cuts: tenure <6/6–18/18–36/36+ (only where first DK deposit is ≥3 months post-entry; censored group separate) and pre-first-Kalshi (DK+FD) deciles, never contemporaneous; never-adopters get pseudo-adoption dates from matched strata; pooled plus ten splits, agreement shown. Headline $ displaced per 10-point share rise, not an elasticity; PPML log-log elasticity separately.

**Validation** (kill criteria pre-set). Permutation null: fails if β is indistinguishable at 5%. Placebos: grocery/streaming/general-merchandise outcomes ≈ 0; non-legal states = error floor; neobank/P2P first funding as fake treatment. Pre-trends: six leads jointly insignificant, p > 0.10. FD symmetry, difference = brand switching. Wallet accounting ±6 months: ΔKalshi, ΔDK, ΔFD, Δother gambling, Δsavings, Δdiscretionary as shares summing to 100. Randomization inference: 1,000 within-user adoption-date reassignments. Panel integrity: ≥95% completeness, drop mid-window card/account switches; >30% move = artifact. Out-of-sample: fit 2024 cohorts, predict 2025 adopters. Power first: MDE = 2.8·sd(ΔDK)/(sd(Δshare)·√N_eff), design effect 1+(m−1)ρ, ρ ≈ 0.05–0.15, in $/10 share points.

**Pitfalls.** Denominator: regressor contains the outcome; test against the permuted null, lead with baseline-deflated. Gross deposits ≠ spend: prefer net funding; Kalshi round-trips capital, so compare deposit dollars only. Deposits ≠ revenue: ~9–11% hold vs a small exchange fee; convert at explicit take rates. User-specific seasonality (NFL-only vs year-round, adoption clustered on elections/events): add user-type × season controls. Common-cause shocks (state taxes, levies, promos, outages): state × month and the instrument cover some; a coincident national DK shock is inseparable, flag it. Selection: bound if pre-trends fail. Attrition: balanced and unbalanced panels, Lee bound if they differ. Whales: dollar- and user-weighted separately, headlined if the top decile dominates.
