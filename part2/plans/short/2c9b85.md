# 2c9b85

Hypothesis: round-lot (100-share multiple) imbalance is tested as a retail proxy, not assumed, since algo slicing and odd-lot rules confound it. Primary: daily signed round-lot order-flow imbalance (RLOIB) predicts negative one-day-ahead returns, attenuating or flipping by day 20. Tradeable version: a long-short book fading RLOIB earns alpha against factors including short-term reversal (STR), surviving a spread-plus-impact cost model. Falsification: if RLOIB's incremental Fama-MacBeth coefficient (controlling for all-lot imbalance ALLOIB and subpenny retail imbalance RETOIB) has |t|<2.0 at every horizon, the lot filter adds nothing; if it survives but net-of-cost long-short alpha has t<2.0 under base costs, the effect is real but unharvestable.

Data: NYSE Daily TAQ (Trades, NBBO, Master) via WRDS, 2 Jan 2015–present (odd lots reportable from Dec 2013); 2010–2013 extension is a caveated stability check. CRSP daily returns/prices/shares/exchange/delisting; Compustat book equity lagged 6 months; Ken French daily 5-factor + momentum + STR; IHS Markit (or Ortex) lending fees; Thomson/Refinitiv 13F ownership, lagged 1 quarter; Compustat/IBES earnings dates. Universe: CRSP codes 10/11, NYSE/NYSE American/Nasdaq, price $5–$1000, mkt cap above NYSE 20th percentile, 20-day median dollar volume >$1M; drop 5 days post-IPO, 2 days around splits/distributions, earnings day/day-after in main spec (kept as robustness).

Methodology: clean trades per Holden–Jacobsen (09:30–16:00, correction 00, conditions blank/@/F/I, drop non-positive price/size and >10% deviations from surrounding 20-print median); NBBO filtered for crossed/locked quotes and wide spreads. Sign via Lee–Ready vs. contemporaneous NBBO (no lag), tick test for midpoints; EMO/CLNV as robustness. Lots: odd <100, round = multiples of 100, mixed >100 non-multiple; primary signal uses round lots plus a "retail-plausible" 100–500-share variant; 100-share threshold held fixed despite SEC's later tiered redefinition. RLOIB = (buy−sell round-lot dollars)/(buy+sell), min 20 prints/$50k volume per stock-day, winsorized 1/99% and z-scored cross-sectionally. Companion signals ALLOIB, ODDOIB, RETOIB (subpenny off-exchange classification, validated against venue retail data) built identically. Identification: residualize RLOIB on ALLOIB, ODDOIB, log price/size, rerun regressions on raw and residual; also regress RLOIB on RETOIB in rolling 250-day windows. Predictive regressions: signal at t, entry t+1, returns to t+1+h (h=1,5,20); controls: day-t return, momentum, size, book-to-market, turnover, Amihud illiquidity, volatility, price, round-lot share, institutional ownership. OLS daily cross-section; Newey-West(h+5) SEs cross-checked against Hansen-Hodrick and block bootstrap. Portfolios: daily decile sort fading imbalance, value-weighted, 5-day overlapping holding, dollar- and beta-neutral; costs via TAQ effective half-spread (Corwin-Schultz fallback) plus square-root impact (k=0.5 base, 0.25/1.0 stress, 5% participation cap), Markit short fees (excluding DCBS≥5 or fee>100bps names), 1bp commission; buy/hold-band turnover control (enter decile 1/10, exit 3/8); breakeven cost vs realized cost. Evaluation regresses net returns on 6 standard factors plus in-house ALLOIB/RETOIB factors; bar for real effect: net alpha t>3.0.

Validation: split at end-2019 (zero-commission transition, 2020-21 retail surge); Jan–Mar 2021 reported separately as stress test. Placebos: 250-750 mixed lots, exact-100-only bucket, 5-day-forward signal shift, within-stock-day sign randomization. Sensitivity grid: signing algorithm, NBBO lag, scaling, smoothing, earnings inclusion, deciles vs quintiles, weighting, impact calibration; midquote and t+2–t+6 returns strip bid-ask bounce. Capacity: net alpha across $50M–$5B AUM.

Pitfalls: round lots aren't a clean retail marker (institutional slicing at 100, odd-lot frequency rises with price) — addressed via residualization, price control, RETOIB correlation. Result may be STR relabeled — day-t return control and the STR factor are non-negotiable. Sign may differ by horizon (1-day = liquidity provision; a positive 20-day coefficient is reinterpreted, not failure). Costs likely dominate given concentration in small/cheap/high-retail names — breakeven cost is a headline output. Short leg faces squeeze/borrow-scarcity risk — report long-only-relative-to-universe and short-leg alpha share. Data traps: TAQ symbol/CUSIP-PERMNO changes across corporate actions, the Dec 2013 odd-lot break, later round-lot tiering, sale conditions reintroducing auction volume, delisting fills (-30%/-55%), quote-alignment lookahead.
