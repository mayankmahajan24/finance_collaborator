# 4148d8

**Hypothesis.** For per-seat vendors, distinct monthly portal logins measure the billed unit: within a company, a 1% change in MAU implies a β% change in ARR one quarter or less later, β positive and residuals smaller than sell-side dispersion. MAU is not seats; the mechanism needs MAU-per-seat and price per seat drifting slowly. Null: MAU adds nothing beyond lagged ARR and company trend.

**Data.** 35–60 listed per-seat names: Atlassian, Asana, Monday, Smartsheet; Salesforce, HubSpot, ZoomInfo; Adobe, Figma; ServiceNow (CrowdStrike per-endpoint, likely excluded); Workday, Bill.com, Paycom, Paylocity; Zoom, RingCentral, Twilio Flex. Excluded: consumption meters (Snowflake, Datadog, MongoDB, Confluent), transaction meters (Shopify, Toast), admin-console portals. Rule fixed beforehand: seat-metered >70% of subscription revenue per 10-K or management, primarily in-browser. Authenticated hostnames only (app.\*, \*.my.\*, wildcard tenant subdomains), never marketing www; confirm wildcard rollup. MAU from distinct-user panels (Similarweb app-level uniques, Datos, Verto, Sensor Tower/data.ai where app usage is material), preferring exposed monthly panel size; two sources where available, disagreement flagging quality. Dependent variable, tier recorded: (1) disclosed ARR/ACV; (2) quarterly subscription revenue annualized; (3) total revenue less professional-services and license revenue; never mixed within a series (splice on an overlap ratio, or truncate). Actuals from 8-Ks, point-in-time stamped; consensus from Visible Alpha line items (subscription revenue, ARR, NRR, billings) at print morning, with contributor count and cross-analyst standard deviation; Bloomberg BEst fallback. Quarterly, MAU over each fiscal quarter's actual dates; ≥20 quarters targeted, under 12 dropped; ~2018 onward, 900–1,000 company-quarters.

**Estimation.** Log ARR on log MAU at h ∈ {0,1,2}, company and quarter indicators, within transformation. All three horizons plus a joint-lag specification, chosen on out-of-sample performance; expect β 0.4–0.8. Guards: CIPS panel unit-root and Westerlund cointegration tests, plus the error-correction form (Δlog ARR on Δlog MAU and the lagged level residual) if cointegrated; re-estimation in year-over-year log differences; company-specific linear trends, trading only the differenced or ECM result if β collapses. Cluster on company; also Driscoll–Kraay (Newey–West lags 2–3), wild cluster bootstrap (Rademacher, 999 reps) for β, Pesaran CD.

**Validation.** Random 20% company-quarter holdout, refit, level MAPE against the stated 3% bar; lead with three identically fit benchmarks: (A) ARR carried forward; (B) grown at trailing four-quarter average sequential growth; (C) two-way fixed effects without MAU. Claim survives only if the MAU model beats C out-of-sample under Diebold–Mariano on absolute-error differentials, clustered by quarter, p < 0.05; report the MAPE ratio. Random splits leak: keep as headline, gate on rolling-origin (train through T, predict T+1, last 12 quarters) and leave-one-company-out. In growth terms: median absolute year-over-year error below 150 bps and below cross-analyst consensus dispersion in ≥60% of company-quarters. Falsification: refit on the excluded consumption-priced names; equal performance sinks the thesis.

**Pitfalls.** Tracker re-basings give 10–30% step changes: screen with Bai–Perron on log levels, cross-check vendor notes, splice on an overlap ratio or drop pre-break history, never trade within two quarters of a break. Shelfware implies asymmetry: interact log MAU with a negative-year-over-year-MAU indicator; if the downside coefficient is smaller, restrict to positive surprises or split regimes. Price and mix drift raise ARR without logins: log price actions from Wayback pricing-page snapshots as a log price index control; else flag and exclude quarters after announced increases. Panel composition: enterprise SSO under-panelling versus SMB, deep-link misattribution, bots, moving desktop/mobile split; include app MAU or restrict to immaterial-mobile names, held fixed. Corporate actions and disclosure changes: event log, two-quarter exclusion either side. Process: ~40 effective clusters; horizon is fitted, so pick on rolling-origin and report all three; backfilled vendor histories need point-in-time snapshots or acknowledged lookahead; expect edge in smaller-cap names, decaying; split performance by year before scaling capital.
